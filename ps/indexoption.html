Overview

The Option Trader is an EdTech platform built specifically for the stock/options trading education niche — a space where existing platforms are either generic course marketplaces (with no trading context) or trading apps with no structured learning path.

Intro

Project Overview

A unified trading platform that helps new and intermediate traders Learn → Practice → Get Mentored → Go Live in one seamless environment.

The Problem

  • ✦ Trading knowledge was scattered across YouTube, Telegram, and paid courses
  • ✦ Beginners had no safe way to practice before using real money
  • ✦ Most platforms offered one-way content with little expert interaction
  • ✦ No structured or reliable learning path existed
  • ✦ No single platform connected learning, practice, mentorship, and live trading

Business Goals

  • ✦ Build a structured learning journey for new and intermediate traders
  • ✦ Let users practice with virtual money before risking real money
  • ✦ Enable 1:1 mentorship with experts
  • ✦ Bring learning, practice, mentorship, and live trading into one platform
  • ✦ Help traders build confidence and skills before entering the real market

Earning Model

  • ✦ Demo trading subscription
  • ✦ Mentorship programs
  • ✦ Course sales

My Role & Ownership

Leading Product End-to-End

As Team Lead and Senior Product Designer, I took full ownership of the product lifecycle across strategy, design, and engineering:

  • • Cross-Surface UX/UI: Led UX research, information architecture, and visual design across all 3 product surfaces (Admin Panel, Website, and Mobile App).
  • • Full-Stack Development: Directed the frontend/backend build and personally engineered core backend logic and website modules.
  • • AI-Assisted Velocity with Antigravity: Used Google Antigravity as an agentic AI development environment to compress build cycles — taking Figma specs directly into functional code while retaining 100% design fidelity.
  • • Zero Handoff Friction: Wearing both designer and developer hats eliminated typical handoff loss and accelerated decision-making.

Solution

Connected ecosystem that brings learning, course discovery, trading practice, and platform management together in one place.

  • ✦ Course Website — Web space where users can browse courses, see what's included, and purchase the ones they want.
  • ✦ Student Mobile App — A learning and trading companion where students can access their courses, join live classes, track progress, and practice trading with the demo feature.
  • ✦ Admin Backend — A central dashboard to manage courses, students, purchases, live sessions, enquiries, and overall platform activity.

Execution Process

Step 1

Stakeholder & PM Discussions
  • • Discussed the vision for the web platform and mobile app with stakeholders and PMs.
  • • Focused on understanding the purpose, the problem it solves, and the target users first.
Step 2

Requirement Gathering
  • • Turned discussions into clear, documented requirements.
  • • Captured functional needs across all three surfaces — Admin Panel, Website, and App.
Step 3

Feature & Module Finalization
  • • Worked with stakeholders and PMs to finalize key features (course management, live classes, appointments, demo trading, etc.).
  • • Prioritized scope together to avoid overbuilding in v1.
Step 4

Low-Fidelity Wireframes
  • • Sketched wireframes to map screen structure, navigation, and user flows for both Instructor and Student journeys.
  • • Kept it rough on purpose — focused on layout and flow, not visuals — to get quick stakeholder buy-in.
Step 5

Visual Design Direction
  • • Finalized the visual language — colors, typography, and components — suited for a fintech/trading product, not a generic course site.
  • • Designed a dashboard-first interface so the demo trading experience feels like a real trading platform.
Step 6

Major Screens in Figma
  • • Designed the key high-priority screens first — course purchase, live class access, appointment booking, and demo trading dashboard — to lock down the design system and interaction patterns.
Step 7

Direct-to-Antigravity for High-Fidelity Execution
  • • Instead of manually building every remaining screen in Figma, we moved directly into Google Antigravity to create high-fidelity screens using the established design system as reference.
  • • This let design and development happen in parallel — speeding up the timeline while staying consistent with the Figma design language.

Backend

01.

Dashboard

A centralized overview providing real-time insights into learners, course sales, revenue, recent activity, and upcoming live sessions, helping admins quickly monitor platform performance and manage key activities.

02.

Course Management

A complete backend interface for creating, editing, and publishing courses — including module structure, video content, pricing, and enrollment controls — all managed from a single workspace.

03.

Learner Management

Track enrolled students, monitor their progress across courses, view purchase history, and manage accounts — giving admins full visibility into the learner base in one place.

04.

Demo Trading Terminal

A realistic trading simulator with live-style charts, order entry panels (Buy/Sell, Limit, Stop Loss), and a virtual portfolio tracker — letting students practise trading with zero financial risk before going live.

05.

Live Sessions

Schedule, manage, and host live trading classes directly within the platform. Students join from the app, while admins control scheduling, notifications, and session recordings from the backend.
